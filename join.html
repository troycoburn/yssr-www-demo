Join Our Volunteer Team

Make an extraordinary difference in your community. Whether searching in the field or supporting operations behind the scenes, there is a place for you on our team.

Volunteer Intake

Two Ways to Get Involved

We welcome new members with diverse backgrounds, skills, and perspectives. You don’t need prior search experience or military background—our certified trainers provide all required education!

1. Operational Ground Searchers

Trained to respond to search callouts in the woods, on shorelines, and in urban environments. Applications for operational members are accepted twice per year around our New Member Nights to coordinate cohort training.

Recruitment Semiannual

2. Non-Operational Specialists

Volunteers who support IT, website, vehicle fleet upkeep, fundraising, grant writing, PR, and administration. You can apply and join at any time throughout the year!

Open Year-Round
Youth Opportunity

Junior Program (Ages 16–17)

Our Junior Program offers youth aged 16–17 an outstanding opportunity to give back to the Fredericton community while receiving valuable search and rescue, wilderness navigation, and first aid training.

Note: Junior members participate in all training exercises and team activities, but are restricted to support roles during live emergency search callouts. A parent or guardian signature is required.

How To Apply

The 4-Step Application Checklist

Follow these clear steps to complete your onboarding package with YSSR.

  1. Step 1: Complete the Required Documentation

    Every applicant must complete the required five forms. You can download and fill them out in advance:

    • YSSR Membership Application Form
    • YSSR Release & Waiver Form
    • Consent for Release of Police Information
    • Criminal Record Check Application
    • Vulnerable Sector Screening Check

    Email completed digital copies to membership@yssr.ca or bring printed copies to our meetings.

  2. Step 2: In-Person Identity Verification

    RCMP background checks require in-person verification of two pieces of government-issued ID (e.g., Driver's License, Passport, NB Medicare card). We verify IDs at our General Meeting (first Wednesday of each month) or prior to Executive Meetings.

  3. Step 3: Background Clearance & Membership Dues

    Once police background checks are clear, new members submit initial dues of $40 (which covers your official YSSR high-visibility uniform shirt). Annual membership dues are $20 each year after.

  4. Step 4: Searcher Training Academy

    Complete our weekend Basic Searcher Course (approx. 15 hours). Within 6 months of becoming operational, obtain or verify your Standard First Aid & CPR certificate (YSSR hosts courses regularly).
