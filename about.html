Who We Are

York Sunbury Search and Rescue is a 100% volunteer-operated, charitable organization dedicated to bringing lost loved ones home safely.

Our Mission

Prepared, Trained, and Ready When Minutes Count

People never head into the outdoors expecting to get lost. Yet in New Brunswick’s dense forests, rugged terrain, and rapidly shifting weather, it is all too easy to become disoriented, injured, or stranded.

When a child, hiker, hunter, mushroom forager, or vulnerable senior goes missing, every hour that passes decreases the probability of survival. That is why York Sunbury Search and Rescue maintains round-the-clock readiness.

We are 1 of 9 Ground Search and Rescue (GSAR) teams in the province of New Brunswick. We work under the direct tasking and authorization of police services, providing specialized personnel, mobile incident command, electronic tracking, and wilderness extraction capabilities.

Core Operating Principles

  • 100% Volunteer: Every member—from field searchers to our Executive Board—is an unpaid community volunteer.
  • 24/7/365 Activation: We respond at midnight, during blizzards, or in the heat of summer upon police request.
  • Inter-Agency Coordination: Seamless interoperability with Fredericton Police, RCMP, NB EMO, and sister GSAR units.
  • Zero Charge to Families: Search and rescue services are always provided free of charge to victims and families.
Coverage & Geography

Where We Operate

While rooted in Fredericton and York & Sunbury counties, our rescue footprint spans throughout New Brunswick and neighboring regions.

Primary Response Zone

City of Fredericton, York County, and Sunbury County. Including communities along the Wolastoq (Saint John) River valley, Oromocto, Hanwell, New Maryland, Stanley, and Harvey.

Provincial Mutual Aid

As part of the NB GSAR network, we respond across New Brunswick to support sister teams in Saint John, Miramichi, Charlotte County, Moncton, Woodstock, and the North Shore.

Regional Deployments

When multi-day large scale searches require specialized tracking or command resources, YSSR has deployed to Nova Scotia, Prince Edward Island, and the State of Maine.

Volunteer Structure

Two Paths to Make an Impact

You don't need to bushwhack in freezing rain to save lives. Our team depends on both field searchers and behind-the-scenes specialists.

Operational Searchers

Field responders trained in compass navigation, GPS, tracking, radio communications, wilderness survival, search theory, and medical extraction. Responds to callouts across rugged terrain.

  • Mandatory weekend searcher academy
  • Standard First Aid & CPR certification
  • Field readiness gear and exercises

Non-Operational Specialists

Essential support volunteers who keep the organization running efficiently. Work in IT, communications, grant writing, vehicle maintenance, PR, event coordination, and bookkeeping.

  • Join anytime throughout the year
  • Flexible commitment based on your skill set
  • No demanding physical fitness criteria
