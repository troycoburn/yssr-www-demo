Project Lifesaver

Protecting vulnerable individuals prone to wandering with proven radio-frequency tracking technology.

Program Overview

A Lifeline for Families and Caregivers

The primary mission of Project Lifesaver International (PLI) is to provide timely response to save lives and reduce potential injury for adults and children who wander due to cognitive conditions such as Alzheimer's disease, dementia, Autism Spectrum Disorder, Down syndrome, or traumatic brain injury.

Enrolled clients wear a lightweight, water-resistant transmitter bracelet on their wrist or ankle. Each transmitter emits an individual, continuous frequency pulse 24 hours a day.

When a family member or caregiver realizes the client has wandered, they immediately call 911. Police notify YSSR’s Electronic Search Specialists (ESS), who arrive equipped with specialized directional antenna receivers to track the signal straight to the individual.

Proven Results

Why Radio Frequency (RF) Beats GPS

GPS devices rely on cellular coverage and clear line-of-sight to orbiting satellites—both of which fail inside buildings, basements, tunnels, dense tree canopies, or remote ravines.

  • Works Indoors & Underground: Radio signals penetrate buildings, foliage, and adverse weather.
  • Long Battery Life: Transmitters run continuously for 30–60 days on a single battery, without needing nightly charging.
  • Tamper-Resistant: Secure hospital-grade wristbands prevent accidental removal.
Operational Process

How Project Lifesaver Works in Action

From client enrollment to emergency activation, we make the process simple and dependable.

  1. 1. Enrollment & Fitting

    The family or guardian contacts our Project Lifesaver coordinator. We complete registration, assign an authorized frequency, and safely fit the personalized bracelet to the client.

  2. 2. Monthly Care & Battery Maintenance

    Every 30 days, batteries and bands are inspected and replaced to maintain continuous 100% operation. Caregivers receive a simple daily tester to confirm the signal is active.

  3. 3. Emergency Callout

    If the loved one wanders, the caregiver dials 911 immediately and informs dispatch that the individual is in Project Lifesaver. YSSR is dispatched simultaneously with emergency police units.

  4. 4. Rapid Directional Location

    Using vehicle-mounted and handheld directional Yagi antennas, YSSR searchers home in on the signal tone. Average recovery times are under 30 minutes, virtually eliminating injury or hypothermia.
