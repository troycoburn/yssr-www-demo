Targeted Impact

Equipment Wishlist

Search and rescue gear undergoes punishing abuse across mud, snow, rain, and rocks. Your donations directly sponsor critical tools.

VHF Radios & GPS Units

Rugged handheld radios and satellite GPS receivers ensure search teams remain connected with Incident Command in deep river valleys.

Approx. $450 – $800 each

Hypothermia Rescue Wraps

Multi-layer patient thermal insulation blankets and active chemical warming pods that prevent hypothermia during prolonged evacuations.

Approx. $250 per kit

Project Lifesaver Transmitters

Waterproof tracking bracelets and testing kits provided to low-income families of children with autism and seniors with dementia.

Approx. $350 per client package

High-Power Scene Floodlights

Portable battery-powered LED scene lighting for establishing landing zones and command posts during dark night operations.

Approx. $600 per light

Charitable Transparency

York Sunbury Search and Rescue Inc. is a registered non-profit and charitable organization in good standing in the Province of New Brunswick.

  • Official tax receipts provided for all donations of $20 or more.
  • 0% spent on executive salaries—100% of leadership is volunteer.
  • Strict oversight by the volunteer Board of Directors and annual CRA reporting.

York Sunbury Search & Rescue

York Sunbury Search and Rescue is a 100% volunteer-operated registered charity dedicated to locating and recovering lost and missing persons 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.
