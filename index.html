How We Serve Our Community

From dense New Brunswick forests to urban searches and public safety education, our team delivers essential search and rescue services.

Trained and equipped to deploy immediately and sustain wilderness and urban searches for multiple days across varied New Brunswick terrain.

Life-Saving Technology

Protecting Loved Ones Who Wander

When a child with autism or an elder with Alzheimer's goes missing, every single minute counts. Project Lifesaver equips vulnerable individuals with a personalized radio transmitter bracelet.

  • Under 30-Minute Search Times: Radio frequency tracking leads searchers directly to the client.
  • Specialized Electronic Searchers: YSSR members are certified Electronic Search Specialists.
  • Community Peace of Mind: Offering proactive security and 24/7 activation support for local families.

How We Respond to a PLI Call

When a 911 dispatch alerts our team to an enrolled client, our responders deploy directly with mobile directional tracking antennas.
